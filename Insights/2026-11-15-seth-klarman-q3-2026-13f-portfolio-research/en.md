Starting with $27 million in initial capital derived from Poorvu’s sale of a local television station, Klarman accepted a starting salary of $35,000 per year, quickly establishing a reputation for exhaustive primary research and rigorous risk management.

Klarman is widely regarded as one of the preeminent modern practitioners of Benjamin Graham’s margin-of-safety value investing framework. To analyze Baupost’s primary exits and trims in Q3 2026, *DeepValues Research* conducted owner-earnings Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) modeling and Reverse DCF calculations based on official SEC XBRL filings (Form 10-K and Form 10-Q).

Owner earnings are defined as:
$$\text{Owner Earnings} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Maintenance Capex} - \text{Stock-Based Compensation}$$

Standard *maintenance capex* is defined as the smaller of total capital expenditures and depreciation & amortization. A secondary, stricter metric—*Owner Earnings After All Capex*—subtracts total reported capex. 

All DCF intrinsic value evaluations apply a **10.0% discount rate**, growing cash flows for Years 1–5 at rate $g$, fading growth linearly to **2.5%** over Years 6–10, and calculating a perpetual terminal value at **2.5%**. Reverse DCF calculations solve for the 5-year growth rate $g$ implied by current market pricing under these exact standard discount and terminal rate parameters.

## Regulatory Disclosures & SEC Form 13F Limitations

### SEC Form 13F Limitations
SEC Form 13F filings require institutional investment managers controlling over $100 million in U.S. equity assets to disclose long equity positions, convertible debt, and options holdings within 45 days of each calendar quarter end. Form 13F filings represent historical snapshots as of September 30, 2026, and do not reflect transactions executed post-quarter end. 13F filings explicitly omit non-U.S. equity securities traded on international exchanges, fixed income securities, distressed credit instruments, private investments, short positions, and cash balances. Consequently, 13F data do not represent total fund performance or comprehensive net risk exposure.
