# Applied Materials Q4 and FY2026 Financial Evaluation: Owner Earnings, AI Demand, and Capital Allocation

Following the release of its Q4 and full fiscal year 2026 financial results on November 12, 2026, Applied Materials, Inc. (AMAT) demonstrated a structural step-up in underlying earnings power, driven by surging capital expenditures across advanced logic and memory nodes for artificial intelligence (AI) computing architectures. The company reported record annual revenue of $34.45 billion—up 21% year-over-year—and full-year non-GAAP net income of $10.30 billion, alongside record operating cash flow of $8.87 billion. For long-term value investors, this expansion reflects deep technological lock-in and pricing power across materials engineering, enhanced by disciplined capital allocation ($4.75 billion returned via buybacks and dividends in FY2026 alongside strategic investments like Besi). However, this baseline cash generation expansion is tempered by friction in working capital—specifically a 73.5% surge in accounts receivable—as well as ongoing U.S. export control compliance oversight and high customer concentration.

## Owner Earnings and Cash Flow Analysis

A value-investing evaluation of Applied Materials requires penetrating GAAP earnings metrics to determine the business's true recurring "Owner Earnings"—the discretionary cash flow remaining after funding all necessary plant, equipment, and technology reinvestments.

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 (ended October 25, 2026), Applied Materials generated GAAP net income of $2.72 billion ($3.40 per diluted share) and non-GAAP net income of $3.32 billion ($4.16 per diluted share). For the full fiscal year 2026, GAAP net income reached $10.09 billion ($12.62 per diluted share), up 44% from $7.00 billion ($8.66 per diluted share) in FY2025. Full-year non-GAAP net income reached $10.30 billion ($12.89 per diluted share), expanding 35% from $7.61 billion ($9.42 per diluted share) in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).

To calculate Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow (Owner Earnings), operating cash flow is adjusted for cash outlays for property, plant, and equipment:
*   **Q4 FY2026 Operating Cash Flow:** $3.30 billion ($3,300 million)
*   **Q4 FY2026 Capital Expenditures:** $684 million
*   **Q4 FY2026 Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow:** $2.62 billion ($2,616 million)
*   **FY2026 Operating Cash Flow:** $8.87 billion ($8,868 million)
*   **FY2026 Capital Expenditures:** $2.67 billion ($2,672 million)
*   **FY2026 Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow:** $6.20 billion ($6,196 million), representing a 8.7% increase over FY2025 Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow of $5.70 billion ($5,698 million, derived from $7.96 billion in operating cash flow minus $2.26 billion in capital expenditures) (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).

A rigorous assessment of cash flow quality demands explicit accounting for Stock-Based Compensation (SBC). In FY2026, SBC totaled $719 million ($174 million in Q4 FY2026), compared to $668 million in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1 Cash Flows). SBC is a real economic expense that dilute existing share owners. Because SBC is already expensed within GAAP Net Income, it must **never** be subtracted a second time from Net Income when assessing statutory net profitability. However, because Cash Provided by Operating Activities adds back SBC as a non-cash expense, investors calculating Owner Earnings starting from Operating Cash Flow must recognize that cash buybacks are required to neutralize this dilution. Of the $3.18 billion spent on share repurchases in FY2026, $719 million (or 22.6%) effectively served merely to absorb SBC dilution, leaving $2.46 billion to reduce the diluted share count from 808 million in FY2025 to 799 million in FY2026.

## Durability of the Moat

Applied Materials’ economic moat relies on broad technological IP across materials engineering, atomic layer deposition, chemical mechanical planarization, eBeam process control, and advanced packaging solutions. 

Operational breakdown by reportable segment illustrates the strength of this moat:
*   **Semiconductor Systems Segment:** Net revenue reached $7.996 billion in Q4 FY2026, up 64% year-over-year from $4.879 billion in Q4 FY2025. Full-year revenue reached $26.142 billion, up 22% year-over-year from $21.441 billion in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1). Effective Q1 FY2026, management moved the 200mm equipment business into Semiconductor Systems from AGS.
*   **Semiconductor Systems End-Market Mix:** Driven by AI scaling, Q4 FY2026 segment sales comprised Foundry/Logic/Other at 58% (vs. 66% in Q4 FY25), DRAM at 33% (vs. 28% in Q4 FY25), and Flash memory at 9% (vs. 6% in Q4 FY25). Full-year FY2026 mix stood at Foundry/Logic/Other at 63%, DRAM at 30%, and Flash memory at 7% (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).
*   **Applied Global Services (AGS) Segment:** Net revenue reached $1.896 billion in Q4 FY2026 (up 26% YoY) with an operating margin of 30.5% ($578 million operating income). Full-year AGS revenue totaled $6.901 billion (up 20% YoY) with an operating margin of 29.5% ($2.039 billion operating income) (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1). AGS provides recurring, subscription-like maintenance, parts, and software revenues tied to AMAT's global installed base. This segment creates high switching costs and generates predictable cash flows that dampen cyclical fluctuations in capital equipment spending.

Applied Materials reinforces its moat through targeted research, development, and engineering outlays:
1.  **R&D Expansion:** RD&E expenses grew to $4.18 billion in FY2026 ($1.13 billion in Q4 FY2026), up from $3.57 billion in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1 Operations).
2.  **EPIC Center Innovation:** Launched the Equipment and Process Innovation and Commercialization (EPIC) Center in Silicon Valley. The company secured four new R&D partnerships (Intel, KIOXIA, BE Semiconductor Industries N.V. [Besi], and UCLA Samueli School of Engineering), bringing total announced engagements to 15 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).
3.  **Product Leadership:** Launched the VeritySEM 20 metrology system, a critical dimension scanning electron microscope (CD-SEM) optimized for high-NA EUV patterning control (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).

Reflecting confidence in this moat, President and CEO Gary Dickerson noted that Applied is "on track to grow our Semiconductor Systems business more than 40 percent in our calendar 2026" (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).

Structural moat risks center on customer concentration and geopolitical exposure. Per the FY2025 Form 10-K, two customers represented 19% and 15% of net revenue, respectively (Source: Form 10-K for FY2025, Note 15). Geopolitical concentration in China remains high: net revenue from China was $2.64 billion (25% of total) in Q4 FY2026 and $9.33 billion (27% of total) for FY2026, compared to $8.53 billion (30% of total) in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1 Geographic Revenue).

## Return on Capital versus Cost of Capital

Applied Materials operates with structural margin expansion and capital efficiency:
*   **Gross Margin:** GAAP gross margin expanded to 50.4% in Q4 FY2026 (50.6% Non-GAAP) and 50.0% for FY2026 (50.1% Non-GAAP), compared to 48.7% GAAP (48.8% Non-GAAP) in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).
*   **Operating Margin:** GAAP operating margin expanded to 35.1% in Q4 FY2026 (35.3% Non-GAAP) and 32.2% for FY2026 (33.1% Non-GAAP), compared to 29.2% GAAP (30.2% Non-GAAP) in FY2025 (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1).

As of October 25, 2026, total stockholders' equity stood at $26.11 billion ($26,106 million), supported by $45.40 billion in total assets. Total debt liabilities comprised $1.30 billion ($1,299 million) in short-term debt and $5.23 billion ($5,233 million) in long-term debt, totaling $6.53 billion ($6,532 million). Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled $9.34 billion ($7.24 billion cash and cash equivalents, $2.10 billion short-term investments) (Source: Q4 FY2026 Earnings Release, EX-99.1 Balance Sheet).

To evaluate operational return quality, Net Invested Capital and Pre-Tax Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) are calculated as follows:
*   **Net Invested Capital:** Total Stockholders' Equity ($26.11 billion) + Total Debt ($6.53 billion) – Cash & Short-Term Investments ($9.34 billion) = **$23.30 billion** ($23,299 million).
*   **Pre-Tax GAAP ROIC:** GAAP Operating Income ($11.09 billion / $11,087 million) ÷ Net Invested Capital ($23.30 billion) = **47.6%**.
*   **Pre-Tax Non-GAAP ROIC:** Non-GAAP Operating Income ($11.42 billion / $11,415 million) ÷ Net Invested Capital ($23.30 billion) = **49.0%**.

A pre-tax ROIC of ~48% against an estimated weighted average cost of capital (WACC) of 8% to 10% confirms that Applied Materials earns returns on capital well in excess of its cost of capital. Return on Equity is driven by high operating margins and operational throughput rather than financial leverage.

## Intrinsic Value versus Price and Margin of Safety

Applied Materials’ intrinsic value is anchored by its position in wafer fab equipment, long-term AI-driven node transitions, high-margin service revenue from AGS, and a clean balance sheet ($9.34 billion in total cash and short-term investments against $6.53 billion in total debt).

However, determining an adequate margin of safety requires evaluating operational and trade risks. Geopolitical enforcement surrounding export licenses, potential foreign export restrictions on key inputs (such as rare earth minerals), customer concentration (34% of FY25 revenue tied to two customers), and WFE capital spending volatility introduce cyclical variation. Investors require a margin of safety that accounts for potential revenue disruptions in China (27% of FY2026 revenue) and potential shifts in customer fab construction schedules.

## Valuation

Evaluating Applied Materials’ intrinsic value involves modeling its Owner Earnings across full semiconductor cycles. In FY2025, the business generated $5.70 billion in Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow ($7.96 billion operating cash flow minus $2.26 billion CapEx). In FY2026, Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow reached $6.20 billion ($8.87 billion operating cash flow minus $2.67 billion CapEx).

A three-case Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) framework using FY2026 baseline cash generation of $6.20 billion outlines potential value outcomes:

1.  **Conservative Growth Case:** Assumes cash flow compound growth slows to 3–5% annually due to tighter trade restrictions, flat WFE capital spending cycles, and sustained facility capital outlays.
2.  **Base Growth Case:** Assumes cash flow compound growth of 8–10% annually, supported by broad growth in Semiconductor Systems (projected by management to grow more than 40% in calendar 2026) and sustained AI-driven demand across leading-edge logic and memory.
3.  **Aggressive Growth Case:** Assumes cash flow compound growth of 12–15% annually, supported by rapid adoption of high-NA EUV metrology, heterogeneous packaging co-optimization, and accelerated growth in AGS high-margin recurring service revenues.

A reverse DCF calculation demonstrates that current equity valuations imply mid-to-high single-digit annual free cash flow growth over the next decade. Given the company's high return profile (pre-tax ROIC of ~48%) and market position, this operational hurdle is achievable, provided investors account for geopolitical risks and working capital absorption.

## What Would Change Our View

The following observable developments would invalidate the positive long-term thesis for Applied Materials:

1.  **Geopolitical & Export Control Escalation:** Enactment of stricter trade controls by U.S. regulatory bodies or retaliatory foreign actions (e.g., expanded restrictions on rare earth minerals) that significantly cut into China revenue (27% of FY2026 net sales).
2.  **Tool-of-Record Displacement:** Failure to retain key Development-Tool-of-Record (DTOR) or Production-Tool-of-Record (PTOR) positions at key foundry/logic or memory customers, resulting in market share loss to competitors.
3.  **Working Capital Deterioration:** Persistent uncoupling of Accounts Receivable growth (up 73.5% in FY2026) relative to net revenue growth (up 21.4%), leading to credit losses or structural cash flow conversion impairment.
4.  **Capital Allocation Missteps:** Failure to earn expected returns on major strategic deployments, including R&D outlays for the EPIC Center or equity investments such as the 20% stake in Besi.
