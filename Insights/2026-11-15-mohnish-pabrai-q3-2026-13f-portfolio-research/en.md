## Closing Note: The Limits of SEC Form 13F Filings

SEC Form 13F reports reflect only US-listed long equity positions held by institutional money managers at the close of the calendar quarter (September 30, 2026) and are submitted up to 45 days after quarter-end. Form 13F filings completely omit short positions, cash holdings, fixed income securities, non-US equities listed on foreign exchanges, derivative instruments, and any trades executed after the quarter concluded.
