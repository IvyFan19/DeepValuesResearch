## 8. Closing Note: The Limitations of 13F Filings

SEC Form 13F filings provide institutional insight into manager position sizing, but they carry inherent structural limitations. Filings are disclosed up to 45 days following the end of each calendar quarter and capture only US-listed long equity positions. They completely omit cash holdings, international equities, fixed-income allocations, short positions, derivatives, and interim portfolio changes executed since quarter-end.
