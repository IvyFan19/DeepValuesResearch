## 4. Intrinsic Valuation of Major Trades: Owner Earnings and DCF Analysis

To evaluate the mathematical rigor behind these capital allocation decisions, DeepValues Research examined SEC XBRL disclosures (Form 10-K and Form 10-Q) for the primary trade allocations. Owner earnings are derived using Warren Buffett's foundational model:

$$\text{Owner Earnings} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Maintenance Capex} - \text{Stock-Based Compensation}$$

*Maintenance Capex* is defined as the lesser of total capital expenditures and depreciation & amortization (D&A). Any capital spending above D&A is isolated as growth capex. Discounted cash flow (DCF) models incorporate a 10.0% discount rate, project owner earnings growth ($g$) for years 1–5, fade growth toward 2.5% across years 6–10, and apply a 2.5% perpetual terminal rate.

## 8. Closing Note on Form 13F Limits and Legal Disclaimer

### Regulatory Limitations of SEC Form 13F Filings
Institutional investment managers exercising investment discretion over $100 million or more in US long equities must submit SEC Form 13F disclosures within 45 days following the end of each calendar quarter. Consequently, the data presented in this analysis reflects portfolio holdings as of September 30, 2026, and does not capture trades, position liquidations, or tactical adjustments executed after quarter-end. Furthermore, 13F disclosures report US-listed long equity positions exclusively; they omit short holdings, cash allocations, foreign equity exchange listings, debt securities, options contracts, and non-reporting asset classes.
