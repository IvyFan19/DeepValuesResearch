## Limits of 13F filings and legal disclaimer

### Structural Limits of Form 13F Disclosures
Form 13F filings provide useful institutional snapshots, but carry inherent limitations:
* **Time Lag:** Filings are submitted up to 45 days after quarter-end. Holdings reflect the portfolio on September 30, 2026, and may have changed prior to public release on November 05, 2026.
* **Incomplete Asset Coverage:** Form 13F mandatorily reports only US-listed long equity positions. It excludes short positions, cash allocations, non-US foreign listings, bonds, options (such as Check Capital's Berkshire call options), and commodities.
* **No Intra-Quarter Visibility:** Positions bought and sold entirely within a calendar quarter are omitted from 13F reports.
