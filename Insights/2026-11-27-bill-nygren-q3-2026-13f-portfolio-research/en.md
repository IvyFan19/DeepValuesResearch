## Mandatory Regulatory Note on 13F Limits

Institutional portfolio analysis based on SEC Form 13F disclosures carries specific technical and regulatory limitations:

1. **Reporting Lag:** SEC Form 13F disclosures represent a snapshot of long equity holdings on the final day of the calendar quarter (September 30, 2026) and are filed up to 45 calendar days later (November 16, 2026). Portfolio positions may have changed significantly since the filing date.
2. **Incomplete Asset Coverage:** Form 13F disclosures cover US-listed long equity securities only. They exclude cash balances, foreign exchange holdings, non-US equity securities, fixed income assets, short positions, and option contracts.
3. **Absence of Trading Context:** Filings provide no visibility into intra-quarter execution, portfolio hedging strategies, or tax-loss harvesting motivations.
