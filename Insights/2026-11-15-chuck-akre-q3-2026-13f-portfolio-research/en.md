Akre’s core framework is defined by his "Three-Legged Stool" investment philosophy. The framework requires every portfolio holding to possess three structural characteristics:
1. **Extraordinary Business Models:** Companies capable of generating high rates of return on equity capital, protected by durable economic moats.
2. **Outstanding Management:** High-integrity executive teams with proven track records of skill and alignment with shareholders.
3. **Reinvestment Runway:** Vast opportunities to reinvest free cash flows back into the business at high rates of return over extended time horizons.

The physical metaphor for this philosophy originates from a three-legged milking stool kept in Akre’s office. Because a milking stool is positioned low to the ground, a farmer sitting on it will not fall far if it tilts. In equity investing, this means that even if macroeconomic disruptions occur, fund investors are protected against permanent loss of capital.

Akre was deeply influenced by Thomas Williams Phelps’s book, *100 to 1 in The Stock Market*, as well as Warren Buffett’s annual shareholder letters. Akre, who has owned shares in Berkshire Hathaway since 1977, displays a framed letter from Buffett on his office wall. Drawing on these concepts, Akre coined the term "compounding machines" to describe business franchises capable of compounding shareholder capital at high rates over decades with minimal risk of permanent capital impairment.

In 2020, Akre stepped back from day-to-day portfolio management responsibilities. Longtime partner Chris Cerrone now manages the firm’s investment book, maintaining the execution of the firm's core methodology.

The strategy is characterized by extreme portfolio concentration and exceptionally low turnover. The firm maintains core positions over long time horizons, as evidenced by holdings in Moody's Corporation (held since 2002), Visa Inc. However, this concentrated approach creates a structural performance drag: the strategy tends to lag market indices during sharp macroeconomic style rotations away from quality growth compounders, such as the market contraction experienced in 2022. ## 4. The Biggest Moves, Valued: Owner-Earnings DCF & Reverse DCF Analysis

To evaluate whether Akre Capital Management's sales were driven by valuation expansion, DeepValues Research analyzed the SEC filings for the fund's top trimmed positions—Mastercard, Visa, and Moody's—using a Buffett-style owner-earnings Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model and Reverse DCF framework.

In this framework, **Owner Earnings** are defined as:
$$\text{Owner Earnings} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Maintenance Capex} - \text{Stock Compensation}$$

*Maintenance Capex* is calculated as the lesser of total capital expenditure and depreciation & amortization. Stock compensation is explicitly subtracted as a real economic expense to shareholders.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     BUFFETT OWNER EARNINGS FORMULA                    |
|                                                                       |
|   Operating Cash Flow                                                 |
|   - Maintenance Capex  (Lesser of Total Capex vs. D&A)                |
|   - Stock Compensation (Subtracted once to reflect share dilution)    |
|   -----------------------------------------------------------------   |
|   = Owner Earnings (Intrinsic Cash Flow Base)                         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 6. What a Patient Investor Can Learn

Akre Capital Management’s quarter-end trading activity offers key insights into portfolio management for long-term compounder-oriented investors:

### 1. Prune Based on Valuation, Not Business Deterioration
High-quality compounders with deep economic moats (e.g., Mastercard, Visa, Moody's) can be trimmed when market prices pull far ahead of intrinsic cash flow generation. Trimming 12% to 16% of these core holdings reflects disciplined risk management rather than a breakdown in the original business thesis.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  TRIMMING VS. SELLING FRAMEWORK                   |
|                                                                   |
|  TRIM FRANCHISE:   Price > Intrinsic Value + Thesis Intact        |
|                    -> Reduce exposure, retain core thesis.        |
|                                                                   |
|  LIQUIDATE FULLY:  Conviction Slipped or Capital Reallocated      |
|                    -> Complete exit (e.g., Airbnb / ABNB).        |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 8. Closing Disclaimer & Limitations of 13F Filings
