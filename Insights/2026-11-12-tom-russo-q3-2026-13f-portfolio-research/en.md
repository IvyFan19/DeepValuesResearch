## 4. The Biggest Moves, Valued

To evaluate whether Tom Russo’s Q3 trimming actions align with fundamental value metrics, DeepValues Research applies Warren Buffett–style Owner Earnings Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and Reverse DCF models derived from ten years of SEC XBRL filings (Form 10-K and 10-Q). 

### Valuation Methodology & Mechanics:
* **Starting TTM Owner Earnings Formula:** Calculated as `Operating Cash Flow - Maintenance Capex - Stock-Based Compensation`.
* **Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) Dilution:** In DeepValues DCF modeling, SBC is explicitly deducted as an operating cash outflow. Although non-cash on the cash flow statement, issuing stock to employees dilutes existing shareholders just as directly as cash compensation. Treating SBC as a free cash expense prevents artificial overstatement of owner earnings.
* **Maintenance Capex vs. Growth Capex:** Maintenance capex is defined as the smaller of total Capital Expenditures (Capex) and Depreciation & Amortization (D&A), representing the baseline capital required to maintain current operational capacity. Any capex spent above D&A is classified as growth investment intended to expand future earnings. If growth capex fails to generate returns above the cost of capital, the stricter valuation model subtracting *all* capex reflects true free cash generation.
* **Discount Rate & Terminal Parameters:** All models utilize a standard 10.0% discount rate. Years 1–5 project owner earnings growth at rate $g$, years 6–10 linearly fade to a perpetual growth rate of 2.5%, followed by a terminal value capitalized at 2.5%.
* **Reverse DCF (Implied Growth Rate):** Solves for the exact annual owner earnings growth rate required over the next 5 years to justify the current market price under standard DCF parameters.
* **Verification Status:** Evaluates data integrity across independent sources. "Double Verified" indicates independent secondary database runs match primary SEC filing runs within 0.0% to 3.0% tolerance.
