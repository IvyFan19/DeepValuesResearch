## The Investor: Philosophy, Fund and Lifetime Work

Bill Gates’s capital allocation history represents one of the most remarkable transitions from aggressive technology entrepreneurship to institutional value compounding and global philanthropic scale in modern economic history. 

### Microsoft Era and Technological Roots
Gates co-founded Microsoft in 1975 alongside Paul Allen, driving the personal computer revolution through microcomputer software standardizations. Throughout his operating tenure at Microsoft—serving as Chief Executive Officer from 1975 to 2000, Chairman of the Board from 1981 to 2014, and Chief Software Architect from 2000 to 2008—Gates managed capital allocation around high gross margins, operating leverage, and software ubiquity. He stepped down as Chairman in 2014 to serve as Technology Advisor to CEO Satya Nadella before fully resigning from Microsoft’s Board of Directors in 2020 to focus entirely on capital deployment via global health and philanthropic vehicles.

### Philanthropic Capital and Institutional Structure
In 1994, Gates established the William H. Gates Foundation using personal Microsoft equity. In 2000, Gates and his then-wife combined three family entities to form the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Following Melinda French Gates’s resignation in 2024, the institution was renamed the Gates Foundation, with Gates serving as sole chair. Having directed $100 billion toward total lifetime charitable causes—including $60 billion directly into the foundation—Gates deploys institutional capital across core development pillars:
*   **Global Health:** Combating transmissible diseases through widespread eradication campaigns targeting polio, malaria, and tuberculosis.
*   **Global Development & Poverty Alleviation:** Expanding water, sanitation, and hygiene infrastructure alongside universal access to consensual family planning.
*   **Agricultural Progress:** Funding agricultural biotechnology, such as the International Rice Research Institute’s Golden Rice project, to address systemic malnutrition.
*   **Education & Open Access:** Elevating domestic digital learning platforms, digital textbook initiatives (OpenStax), and university scholarship grants.

Outside public equity mandates reported via 13F filings, Gates directs personal wealth, private investments, and venture capital through Cascade Investment LLC (headquartered in Kirkland, Washington), BENlabs, TerraPower, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, and Gates Ventures.

## Closing Note: Limits of 13F Filings

Institutional Form 13F filings offer an exceptional window into the public capital allocation decisions of institutional investment managers, but their structural boundaries must be fully understood:
*   **Backward-Looking Reporting:** SEC 13F filings reflect historical equity positions on the final calendar day of the quarter and can be submitted up to 45 days after quarter-end, meaning reported positions may have been altered prior to publication.
*   **Asset Coverage Exclusions:** Regulatory filings report exclusively long US equity positions and convertible securities. Form 13F explicitly excludes cash reserves, fixed-income securities, global equities listed on non-US exchanges, short positions, and private equity investments (such as Cascade Investment's 242,000 acres of direct US farmland ownership, TerraPower, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, or private corporate stakes like Mologic).
