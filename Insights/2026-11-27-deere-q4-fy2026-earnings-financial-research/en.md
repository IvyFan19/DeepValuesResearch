## Owner Earnings and Cash Flow Quality vs. Adjusted EPS

To determine Deere & Company’s underlying cash-generating capacity, value investors must evaluate Warren Buffett’s "Owner Earnings" framework rather than relying on management’s adjusted EPS figures. Owner Earnings measures true cash available to equity owners by adjusting cash flow from operations for net capital expenditures and working capital shifts while properly accounting for non-cash expenses.

When conducting this accounting analysis, share-based compensation expense ($116 million for the nine months ended August 2, 2026, compared to $104 million for the nine months ended July 27, 2025) is a real economic cost already deducted from Net Income in the Statement of Consolidated Income. Therefore, financial analysts must subtract stock compensation *only* when calculating Owner Earnings starting from operating cash flow, and *never* subtract it from Net Income, avoiding double-counting or misrepresenting non-cash employee compensation costs.

For the nine months ended August 2, 2026, Net Income Attributable to Deere & Company stood at $3,808 million (down from $3,962 million in 9M 2025), while Q3 2026 Net Income Attributable to Deere was $1,379 million (up from $1,289 million in Q3 2025). 

Synthesizing Deere's true Owner Earnings for the nine months ended August 2, 2026:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities} &= \$3,250\text{ million} \\
\text{Less: Purchases of Property and Equipment} &= -\$716\text{ million} \\
\text{Less: Share-Based Compensation Expense} &= -\$116\text{ million} \\
\hline
\mathbf{\text{9M 2026 Owner Earnings}} &= \mathbf{\$2,418\text{ million}}
\end{aligned}$$

Comparing this $2,418 million in Owner Earnings against GAAP Net Income Attributable to Deere & Company of $3,808 million reveals that true owner cash generation was approximately 36.5% lower than GAAP net income over the first nine months of FY2026. This disconnect is driven primarily by working capital expansion:

*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Net cash provided by operating activities for 9M 2026 was $3,250 million, down 6.2% from $3,464 million in 9M 2025.
*   **Capital Expenditures:** Purchases of property and equipment absorbed $716 million during 9M 2026, compared to $852 million in 9M 2025.
*   **Working Capital Friction:** The primary cash flow drag stemmed from receivables related to sales, which created a net cash outflow of $1,252 million for 9M 2026, compared to an outflow of $494 million for 9M 2025. This indicates that sales collection speed slowed relative to revenue recognition, tying up substantial liquid capital.
