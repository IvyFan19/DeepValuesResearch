## 9. Structural Limitations of 13F Filings

Form 13F disclosures operate under specific operational and structural constraints that investors must consider:
*   **Reporting Lag**: SEC Form 13F filings are published up to 45 days after the close of each calendar quarter (Q3 ended September 30, 2026; filed November 16, 2026). Holdings may have changed significantly prior to public disclosure.
*   **Incomplete Asset Coverage**: Disclosures reflect US-listed long equity holdings only. They completely omit cash reserves, fixed income securities, short positions, options/derivatives, non-US listings traded on foreign exchanges, and intra-quarter trades.
