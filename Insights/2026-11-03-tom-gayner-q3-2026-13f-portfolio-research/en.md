### 8. Closing Note: The Limitations of 13F Filings & Disclaimer

SEC Form 13F filings provide valuable window insight into institutional capital allocation, but suffer from inherent reporting limitations. Form 13F reflects only long US-listed equity positions as of September 30, 2026, and is published up to 45 days after the close of the calendar quarter. It excludes non-US equity securities, cash balances, fixed-income instruments, short positions, derivatives, and any portfolio transactions executed subsequent to quarter-end.
