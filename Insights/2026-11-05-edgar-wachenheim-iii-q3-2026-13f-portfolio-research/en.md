### Background & Career
Edgar "Ed" Wachenheim III (born 1937 in New York City) grew up in New Rochelle, New York. He began his higher education with two years at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) before transferring to Williams College, where he earned his Bachelor of Arts in 1959. Following an early stint at IBM, Wachenheim attended Harvard Business School, earning his Master of Business Administration (MBA) between 1964 and 1966. He subsequently joined Goldman Sachs as a securities analyst.

In 1969, Wachenheim entered the investment division of Central National-Gottesman (CNG), a firm led by his in-laws, working under legendary investor Arthur Ross. Upon Ross's retirement in 1979, Wachenheim assumed leadership of CNG's investment division. In 1987, he spun off the investment arm to establish Greenhaven Associates, Inc., based in Purchase, New York. Greenhaven initially managed capital exclusively for CNG and the Wachenheim family before expanding its client base to include affluent families, university endowments, and non-profit organizations. Wachenheim continues to serve as CEO and Chairman of Greenhaven Associates while remaining Vice Chairman of the Board of Central National-Gottesman.

Wachenheim seeks out common stocks of well-managed companies trading at substantial discounts to their intrinsic values, requiring a minimal risk of permanent loss of capital while targeting annual returns of 15% to 20%.
