## 4. The Biggest Moves, Valued: Owner Earnings & DCF Analysis

To evaluate the intrinsic valuation mechanics driving Oriental Harbor's major transactions, DeepValues Research executed its standard owner-earnings Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and reverse DCF models across ten years of audited SEC filings (Form 10-K and 10-Q).

**DeepValues Methodology Formula:**
$$\text{Owner Earnings} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Maintenance Capex} - \text{Stock-Based Compensation}$$
*Where Maintenance Capex is defined as the lesser of total Capital Expenditures and Depreciation & Amortization. Stock-based compensation is deducted as a real economic expense.*

*   **Model Parameters:** Discount Rate = 10.0%; Growth years 1–5 = $g$; Years 6–10 fade to a perpetual terminal growth rate of 2.5%. Balance sheet net cash (cash & marketable securities minus total debt) is added to enterprise value to determine total equity intrinsic value.
