## 4. Navigating the AI Wave: Circle of Competence and Commoditization Risks

Navigating technological transformations requires separating temporary supply bottlenecks from durable competitive moats.

**Hyperscaler Prisoner's Dilemma:** Duan frames big tech AI capital expenditure (Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon) through the lens of a classic Prisoner's Dilemma. Even if immediate AI monetization remains unproven and multi-billion-dollar CapEx burdens compress quarterly Free Cash Flow, hyperscalers are strategically obligated to invest aggressively in AI compute infrastructure. Failing to invest creates an existential risk of permanent competitive obsolescence if a fundamental computing platform shift occurs.

**Compute Rental vs. Structural Moats:** Duan draws a sharp distinction between short-term hardware scarcity and multi-year structural economics, particularly regarding AI compute leasing entities like CoreWeave. Over a 1-to-3 year horizon, acute hardware shortages allow compute rental firms to generate substantial short-term earnings. However, over a 3-to-5 year horizon, as global compute capacity scales, raw processing power faces commoditization. Without proprietary software ecosystems or direct customer distribution networks, compute leasing risks devolving into severe price wars—mirroring the solar panel industry, albeit with significantly higher capital entry barriers. Consequently, as noted in *商业模式分级*, Duan refuses to hold compute rental models over a 10-year horizon.

**Hardware Barriers (NVIDIA & HBM):** While cautious on compute leasing, Duan respects high-barrier technology hardware. Rebutting premature "AI bubble" claims, he points to NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang’s 15-year strategic execution in accelerated computing. Furthermore, as detailed in *商业模式分级*, he contrasts low-barrier solar panel manufacturing with High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) technology: while solar components suffer from extreme product homogenization, HBM involves complex fabrication processes and technical barriers to entry that help shield manufacturers from immediate commoditization.

**Circle of Competence Discipline:** Duan’s historical handling of Netflix (NFLX) demonstrates rigorous circle-of-competence discipline.
