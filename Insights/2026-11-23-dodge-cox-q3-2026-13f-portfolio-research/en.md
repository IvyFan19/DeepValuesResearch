$$\text{Owner Earnings} = \text{Operating Cash Flow} - \text{Maintenance Capex} - \text{Stock-Based Compensation}$$

Maintenance Capex is defined as the lesser of total capital expenditure and depreciation & amortization (reflecting the capital required to maintain existing operating capability). All valuation models utilize a standard 10.0% discount rate, a 5-year discrete growth period ($g$), a 5-year transition fade to 2.5%, and a perpetual terminal growth rate of 2.5%.

### 13F Clone Methodology Caveats

1. **Disclosure Lag:** Positions are established at the close of the first trading day following public SEC disclosures (up to 45 days after quarter end), missing intraday price moves.
2. **Pricing Coverage:** The clone tracks U.S. common stocks with available market pricing, covering an average of 87.9% of total reported filing value. Non-priced assets are dropped and remaining holdings reweighted.
3. **Excluded Capital Structures:** The 13F clone excludes corporate bonds, international equity listings, cash reserves (which constitute a portion of overall firm AUM), short positions, options, and trades executed and closed within a single quarter.
