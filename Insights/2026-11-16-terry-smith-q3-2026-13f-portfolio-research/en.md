### 13F Clone Limitations & Structural Differences
A 13F clone is an imperfect proxy for actual fund performance. It exclusively tracks US-listed long equities, omitting short positions, cash reserves, primary listings outside the US (such as UK and European stocks), corporate bonds, options strategies, and intraday trades made between filing dates. Furthermore, all 13F disclosures carry an inherent regulatory reporting lag of up to 45 days post quarter-end. Over the 10-year tracking period, price coverage averaged 96.8% of Fundsmith's reported value; unpriced positions were dropped and remaining assets reweighted.
