# **Section 1: Baupost Group Q4 2025 Portfolio Overview**

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the Form 13F-HR filing submitted by The Baupost Group LLC/MA (CIK: 0001061768\) for the fourth quarter of 2025, covering the period ending December 31, 2025\.1 The filing was accepted by the Securities and Exchange Commission on February 13, 2026\.1

## **1.1 Executive Summary of Q4 2025 Activity**

The fourth quarter of 2025 saw a substantial expansion in Baupost’s reported equity assets and a dramatic shift in its mega-cap technology strategy.

* **Portfolio Value:** The reported value of Baupost’s 13F portfolio rose to **$5.28 billion**.1 This is an increase of roughly 11% from the $4.76 billion reported at the end of Q3 2025\.5  
* **Portfolio Concentration:** The portfolio remains highly concentrated among 22 total holdings.2 The top five positions—Restaurant Brands International (QSR), Amazon (AMZN), Willis Towers Watson (WTW), Elevance Health (ELV), and Union Pacific (UNP)—account for **43.73%** of the total portfolio value.2  
* **Key Thematic Shifts:**  
  1. **A Massive New Tech Anchor:** The defining move of the quarter was the initiation of a **$489.7 million** (9.28%) position in **Amazon (AMZN)**.2 Amazon instantly became Klarman’s second-largest holding, marking a significant return to the stock after having last held it in mid-2023.9  
  2. **Aggressive Scaling of Existing Favorites:** Klarman doubled down on several high-conviction ideas from the previous quarter, most notably increasing his stake in **Fiserv (FISV)** by **\+145.8%** and **Eagle Materials (EXP)** by **\+26.1%**.10  
  3. **The Great Infrastructure/Materials Harvest:** In a sharp reversal of the prior quarter's build-up, Klarman executed a massive **\-68.3%** cut to his position in **CRH PLC (CRH)**.10 He also trimmed other winners like **Alphabet (GOOG)** by **\-41.5%** and **Dollar General (DG)** by **\-22.7%**.10  
  4. **Healthcare Expansion:** While slightly trimming Elevance, Klarman added a new **$108.5 million** position in **Molina Healthcare (MOH)**, further diversifying his exposure to the managed care sector.10

**Section 2: Strategy Analysis and "Battle of the Oracles" Update**

## **2.1 The New Tech Divide: Klarman Buys Amazon as Buffett Sells**

In Q3 2025, the market focused on their divergence over Alphabet. In Q4 2025, a new and sharper divergence emerged regarding **Amazon (AMZN)**.13

* **Klarman's Bullish Re-entry:** Baupost "piled into" Amazon, making it the fund's second-largest holding at 9.28% of assets.9 Klarman likely sees "intriguing value" in Amazon's high-margin segments—AWS and advertising—where growth is accelerating.9 By late 2025, Amazon was valued at roughly 21x forward earnings, which represents a compelling valuation for a dual-industry leader in e-commerce and cloud infrastructure.9  
* **Buffett’s Massive Exit:** Conversely, Warren Buffett’s Berkshire Hathaway slashed its Amazon stake by **\-77%** during the same quarter, reducing its holding from 10 million shares to just 2.27 million.13  
* **Analysis:** This highlights a shift in "Margin of Safety" definitions. Buffett appears to be rotating out of a stock that has realized significant gains, while Klarman is rotating *in*, betting that the market is still underestimating the AI-driven upside for AWS, which grew sales by 13% in Q3.15

## **2.2 The Rotation of "Winners"**

Klarman's Q4 activity was characterized by a "ringing of the register" on previous high-conviction bets that hit their value targets.

* **The CRH Liquidation:** After championing CRH’s U.S. listing and infrastructure moat in previous quarters, Klarman cut the position by **\-68%**.10 This suggests the "re-rating" he anticipated has likely occurred, and he is recycling that capital into newer ideas like Amazon and Molina Healthcare.10  
* **The Continued Alphabet Pare-down:** For the second consecutive quarter, Klarman slashed his **GOOG** stake (this time by **\-41.5%**).9 While he remains a long-term believer in the company’s fundamentals, the stock doubled in price between May 2025 and year-end, leading to a "disciplined profit-taking" move as the valuation hit 23x forward earnings.9

**Section 3: Buffett-Style Analysis of Top Holdings**

## **3.1 Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN) (9.28% of Portfolio; New Holding)**

### **Business & Economic Moat**

* **Moat Assessment: Wide** 15 Amazon possesses a multi-layered moat consisting of **Network Effects** (marketplace), **Cost Advantages** (massive logistical scale), and **High Switching Costs** (AWS infrastructure and Prime ecosystem).9  
* **Moat Trend: Widening.** AWS is increasingly integrated with generative AI and large language models (LLMs), which drove 48% revenue growth in its cloud-related segments in Q4 2025\.9

### **Management & Capital Allocation**

* **Capital Allocation:** Management remains focused on high-margin reinvestment. Operating cash flow grew 36.8% YoY to $35.53 billion by Q3 2025\.15 The company maintains a fortress balance sheet with $66.9 billion in cash and zero short-term debt.15

### **Valuation & Margin of Safety**

* **Valuation:** Klarman’s re-entry at 21x forward EPS suggests he believes the market is underpricing the "foundational cash cow" potential of AWS.9 Analysts have a median price target of **$295.05**, implying roughly 20% upside from year-end levels.15

## **3.2 Restaurant Brands International (QSR) (10.44% of Portfolio; \-2.1% Trim)**

### **Business & Economic Moat**

* **Moat Assessment: Narrow.** Moat is built on iconic brands (Burger King, Tim Hortons) and an asset-light franchise model.16

### **Management & Financials**

* **Financial Health:** The primary "un-Buffett" trait is the high leverage, with a Net Debt-to-EBITDA ratio over 6.0x.16  
* **Baupost's View:** Despite the high debt, Klarman maintained this as his \#1 position, betting on the successful execution of the 5-year growth plan through 2028\.16

## **3.3 Elevance Health (ELV) (8.43% of Portfolio; \-3.8% Trim)**

### **Business & Economic Moat**

* **Moat Assessment: Wide.** Derived from massive scale (117M members) and the regulatory "moat" of BCBS licenses.16

### **Financials & Valuation**

* **Financial Health:** ROE of 13.3% and a strong $199B TTM revenue.17  
* **Valuation:** Remains deeply undervalued from a Buffett perspective. Trading at a P/E of **13.8x** 17, it is significantly below its intrinsic value, estimated by some DCF models at **$588.8** (implying 70%+ undervaluation).18

## **3.4 Union Pacific (UNP) (7.13% of Portfolio; \+8.8% Add)**

### **Business & Economic Moat**

* **Moat Assessment: Wide.** A textbook natural duopoly with a 30,000-mile network that is impossible to replicate.19

### **Management & Profitability**

* **Capital Allocation:** Management recently signed a record **$1.2 billion** locomotive modernization deal, signaling a focus on long-term efficiency and "moat maintenance".20  
* **Financials:** Boasts a 40.9% operating margin and an exceptional **40.4% Return on Equity**.22 It trades at a 21.7x P/E, which analysts view as fair for a "wonderful company".22

## **3.5 Fiserv Inc. (FISV) (2.80% of Portfolio; \+145.8% Add)**

### **Business & Economic Moat**

* **Moat Assessment: Narrow to Wide.** Moat is built on extreme **Customer Switching Costs**; as the "central nervous system" for bank processing, switching is risky and expensive.23

### **Financials & Valuation**

* **Profitability:** Strong operating margins of **28.7%** and a conservative Debt/Equity ratio of 0.88.24  
* **The Catalyst:** The stock faced a 5% decline in late 2025 due to weaker 2026 guidance, creating a "bargain" entry point for Klarman to more than double his stake at an attractive cash flow yield of **14%**.25

**Section 4: Concluding Analysis**

The Q4 2025 filing shows Seth Klarman evolving his "physical world" infrastructure theme into a "digital infrastructure" theme. By rotating out of **CRH** and **Alphabet** and aggressively into **Amazon** and **Fiserv**, he is targeting wide-moat, cash-generative technology and payment platforms that are currently trading at lower multiples than the broader high-growth AI sector.  
Klarman remains a "Value Realizer," harvesting massive gains in Alphabet and CRH to fund new, high-conviction bets in sectors where he sees "asymmetric risk/reward" (Amazon and Elevance Health).26 His portfolio remains a hybrid of Buffett-style quality and Klarman-style opportunistic value.
