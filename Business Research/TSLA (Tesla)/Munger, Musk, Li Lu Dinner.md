# Analysis of Reported Interactions Between Li Lu, Charlie Munger, and Elon Musk

## I. Executive Summary

This report addresses an inquiry regarding potential comments made by investor Li Lu concerning a dinner purportedly attended by himself, the late Charlie Munger (former Vice-Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway), and Elon Musk (CEO of Tesla, Inc.). The objective was to verify the occurrence of such a dinner and ascertain if Li Lu publicly discussed any conversations held during that specific event.

The core finding of this analysis, based on the available information, is that there is no confirmation of a specific dinner event attended simultaneously by Li Lu, Charlie Munger, and Elon Musk. Consequently, there are no public statements or quotes attributed to Li Lu discussing conversations from such a specific gathering found within the examined materials.

However, the investigation provides significant contextual information relevant to the query. It confirms a well-documented, close, and long-standing personal and professional relationship between Charlie Munger and Li Lu, characterized by regular private meetings (dinners or breakfasts) over many years.<sup>1</sup> It also confirms at least one known interaction-a lunch-between Charlie Munger and Elon Musk, during which Munger reportedly offered a critical assessment of Tesla's prospects.<sup>5</sup> Furthermore, the materials extensively document Li Lu's profound respect for Munger and his frequent public discussions of Munger's investment philosophy, wisdom, and mentorship, often drawing on their long association.<sup>1</sup>

This report proceeds by examining the evidence concerning the purported dinner, analyzing the known, separate interactions between the individuals involved, discussing the nature and focus of Li Lu's public commentary (particularly regarding Munger), and evaluating the likelihood of public disclosure concerning any private conversations, considering factors such as privacy norms and competitive sensitivities (notably the Tesla vs. BYD dynamic).

## II. Investigating the Purported Munger-Musk-Li Lu Dinner

### A. Direct Evidence Analysis

A thorough review of the provided information yields no direct confirmation of a dinner event where Li Lu, Charlie Munger, and Elon Musk were all present together. None of the documents examined (covering interviews, speeches, articles, and transcripts related to these individuals <sup>6</sup>) contain any mention or description of such a specific three-person gathering. This absence of direct evidence is a primary finding and directly addresses the foundational aspect of the inquiry - the verification of the event itself. While the absence of evidence does not definitively prove the event never occurred, it establishes that it is not documented or referenced within this corpus of information.

### B. Related Interactions: The Munger-Musk Lunch

While a three-way dinner remains unconfirmed, the materials do corroborate a specific interaction between Charlie Munger and Elon Musk. References indicate that Musk publicly recounted an occasion where he had lunch with Munger. During this lunch, Munger reportedly provided a detailed critique of Tesla, outlining numerous reasons why he believed the company would fail.<sup>5</sup> A specific question regarding this interaction was also noted in materials related to a Daily Journal shareholder meeting, referencing an interview where Musk discussed this lunch and Munger's negative assessment of Tesla.<sup>6</sup>

This confirmed Munger-Musk lunch provides important context. It demonstrates Munger's willingness to engage directly and candidly, even critically, with prominent entrepreneurs like Musk. The reported focus of the conversation was squarely on the potential vulnerabilities and challenges facing Tesla. Significantly, the accounts of this Munger-Musk lunch do not mention Li Lu being present.<sup>5</sup> This suggests it was likely a separate event from the regular interactions Munger had with Li Lu.

### C. Related Interactions: The Munger-Li Lu Dinners

In contrast to the apparently specific and critical nature of the Munger-Musk lunch, the interactions between Charlie Munger and Li Lu were characterized by regularity, depth, and a strong element of mentorship over a prolonged period. Li Lu met Munger on Thanksgiving in 2003, marking the beginning of a close friendship and partnership.<sup>2</sup> Li Lu himself described their subsequent routine: "Mr. Munger and I have been investment partners for 16 or 7 years. We have dinner together at least once a week" (statement contextually dated around late 2021).<sup>1</sup> In a later interview reflecting on Munger after his passing, Li Lu elaborated, stating that after Munger's wife passed away, they shifted their routine to having dinner together every Tuesday, a practice that continued for over a decade.<sup>3</sup>

These regular meetings (initially breakfasts, later Tuesday dinners) served as a forum for wide-ranging discussions. Li Lu described these sessions as opportunities where they "talked about everything," leaving him with "many warm memories" and considerable learning.<sup>3</sup> The context provided by Li Lu emphasizes the role of these interactions in his personal and professional development, framing Munger as a mentor, role model, and source of inspiration.<sup>3</sup> While these frequent, private dinners represent potential settings where various topics, including other business figures or companies, could theoretically have been discussed, the available accounts do not indicate that Elon Musk joined these regular Munger-Li Lu meetings. The described focus remained on the direct relationship and dialogue between Munger and Li Lu.

### D. Analysis of Section II Findings

The examination of interactions reveals a mismatch between the user's query and the documented events. The evidence points distinctly towards two separate types of interactions involving Munger: a specific, critical business lunch with Elon Musk focused on Tesla <sup>5</sup>, and regular, long-term, mentorship-oriented dinners with Li Lu covering broad topics.<sup>1</sup> There is no documented overlap placing all three individuals at the same table in the provided materials.

Furthermore, the nature of these interactions appears different. The Munger-Musk lunch, as recounted by Musk, involved a direct, unvarnished assessment of business risks.<sup>5</sup> In contrast, Li Lu's descriptions of his time with Munger emphasize learning, partnership, and profound personal influence, suggesting a more philosophical and nurturing dynamic.<sup>3</sup> This difference in tone and apparent purpose underscores the distinct relationships Munger maintained.

While the possibility of an unrecorded private dinner involving all three cannot be entirely dismissed based solely on this information set, its absence from any account is significant. Given Li Lu's extensive public commentary on Munger and their relationship, particularly in reflective pieces <sup>3</sup>, the lack of even an oblique reference to a potentially notable dinner with Musk makes it less probable that such an event, if it occurred, was considered significant enough by Li Lu for public mention, or perhaps it was deemed strictly private.

## III. Li Lu's Public Commentary: Focus on Munger, Absence on Queried Dinner

### A. Li Lu's Extensive Commentary on Charlie Munger

The provided materials contain a substantial body of public commentary from Li Lu regarding Charlie Munger, reflecting a deep and multifaceted relationship. Li Lu consistently portrays Munger not only as an investment partner but also as a crucial mentor, a close friend, and even a "father figure".<sup>2</sup> He frequently highlights Munger's defining characteristics, such as his extraordinary patience (illustrated by the anecdote of reading Barron's for decades to find a single investment opportunity), combined with decisive action when opportunities arose.<sup>1</sup>

Li Lu credits Munger with profoundly shaping his own investment approach, particularly in expanding upon the foundational principles of value investing taught by Benjamin Graham.<sup>3</sup> He acknowledges Munger's influence in guiding Himalaya Capital towards long-term value investing <sup>4</sup> and specifically cites Munger's crucial role in understanding and championing the investment in BYD, a Chinese electric vehicle and battery manufacturer.<sup>3</sup> Beyond finance, Li Lu emphasizes Munger's role as an ethical guide and a model for lifelong learning and rational thinking.<sup>3</sup> These tributes and lessons appear across various platforms, including speeches at universities, interviews, and written commemorations <sup>1</sup>, demonstrating Li Lu's consistent effort to share the wisdom gained from his long association with Munger.

### B. Absence of Commentary on the Specific Munger-Musk Dinner

Despite the breadth and depth of Li Lu's public discussions about Charlie Munger, a careful review confirms the absence of any reference to the specific dinner scenario involving Elon Musk outlined in the initial query. Across numerous instances where Li Lu reflects on Munger, their conversations, and the lessons learned <sup>1</sup>, there is no mention of this particular event, nor are there any quotes attributed to Li Lu regarding conversations held during such a hypothetical meeting. This silence is noteworthy given the volume and detail often present in Li Lu's commentary on Munger.

### C. Analysis of Section III Findings

The pattern of Li Lu's public communications about Munger reveals a clear focus. His priority appears to be conveying the enduring lessons, philosophical insights, investment principles, and character traits exemplified by Munger.<sup>1</sup> The content centers on mentorship, ethics, and the practical application of value investing concepts. There is a discernible lack of focus on recounting specific social engagements or detailing the blow-by-blow of private conversations, especially those involving prominent third parties like Musk. This suggests Li Lu's public discourse is intentionally directed towards Munger's intellectual and ethical legacy rather than social reportage.

Furthermore, the absence of comment on the queried dinner could reflect professional discretion. Li Lu manages Himalaya Capital, a fund in which the Munger family invested significantly.<sup>2</sup> Discussing private conversations, particularly those that might involve sensitive business opinions-such as Munger's critical views on Tesla shared with Musk <sup>5</sup>-could be considered imprudent or a breach of trust. Given Li Lu's generally low public profile despite his success <sup>4</sup>, maintaining confidentiality aligns with his observed communication style.

Therefore, the investigation must acknowledge that the premise of the initial query-that such a dinner occurred and Li Lu commented on it-is not supported by the available evidence. The lack of confirmation and commentary is itself a key outcome of this analysis, highlighting the need to base conclusions strictly on documented information rather than assumptions.

## IV. Likelihood of Public Disclosure and Contextual Factors

### A. Privacy Norms in High-Level Interactions

Private dinners and conversations among high-profile business leaders are generally presumed to be off-the-record unless participants explicitly decide otherwise. This contrasts sharply with public forums like shareholder meetings <sup>13</sup>, speeches <sup>11</sup>, or formal interviews <sup>3</sup>, where statements are intended for a wider audience. The fact that Munger and Li Lu maintained a routine of private weekly dinners for over a decade <sup>1</sup> strongly suggests a preference for this confidential setting for substantive, candid exchanges. Any inclusion of another high-profile individual like Musk into such a setting would likely fall under the same expectation of privacy.

### B. Munger's and Li Lu's Communication Styles

Charlie Munger was known for his forthrightness and willingness to share his views, often colorfully, in public settings like the Daily Journal and Berkshire Hathaway annual meetings.<sup>6</sup> However, his documented comments regarding the lunch with Musk appear to have surfaced primarily through Musk's recounting <sup>5</sup> and in response to direct questions during Q&A sessions <sup>6</sup>, rather than Munger proactively publicizing the details of their private conversation.

Li Lu, conversely, presents a communication style marked by deep respect for Munger and a focus on conveying broader principles and philosophical lessons derived from their interactions.<sup>3</sup> His public statements tend towards the educational and analytical, avoiding gossip or detailed accounts of specific social events. His maintenance of a relatively low public profile further supports the notion that he is unlikely to publicly disclose details of private conversations.<sup>4</sup>

### C. The BYD vs. Tesla Dynamic

An important contextual layer is the competitive relationship between Tesla and BYD, particularly concerning Munger and Li Lu. Li Lu is widely recognized for identifying BYD early and bringing the investment opportunity to Munger, resulting in a highly successful investment for Berkshire Hathaway and Himalaya Capital.<sup>3</sup> Munger became a strong advocate for BYD within Berkshire. Simultaneously, Munger expressed significant skepticism about Tesla's valuation and long-term prospects, even directly to Musk.<sup>5</sup>

Given Li Lu's deep involvement with BYD, publicly discussing any interaction involving Munger and the CEO of BYD's primary global competitor, Tesla, would be navigating sensitive territory. It could potentially involve commenting on Munger's contrasting views of the two companies or revealing aspects of private discussions relevant to this competitive landscape. This inherent sensitivity provides a strong additional rationale for discretion on Li Lu's part regarding any such potential meeting.

His public criticism of Tesla alongside his enthusiastic backing of BYD suggests a comparative judgment based on factors like valuation, perceived competitive advantages (moats), management, and risk assessment, rather than a blanket view on the electric vehicle industry itself. ## V. Conclusion

The investigation into comments made by Li Lu regarding a purported dinner with Charlie Munger and Elon Musk concludes that, based on the analyzed materials, there is no verifiable evidence that such a specific three-person dinner occurred. Critically, there is no public record of Li Lu making any statements or providing quotes about conversations held during such a hypothetical event.

While the specific anecdote sought remains unconfirmed, the analysis provides valuable context regarding the relationships and interactions among these individuals. It confirms a known lunch meeting between Munger and Musk where Munger critically assessed Tesla's prospects.<sup>5</sup> It also extensively documents the deep, long-term mentorship and partnership between Munger and Li Lu, characterized by regular private meetings and Li Lu's profound public respect for Munger's wisdom and principles.<sup>1</sup>

The absence of public commentary from Li Lu on the queried dinner is likely attributable to several converging factors: the general expectation of privacy surrounding such high-level private meetings, Li Lu's communication style which prioritizes philosophical lessons over social reporting and maintains a low public profile <sup>3</sup>, and the inherent sensitivities related to the competitive dynamic between Tesla and BYD, a company central to the Munger-Li Lu investment relationship.<sup>4</sup>

Ultimately, the lack of documented evidence regarding the specific dinner and Li Lu's comments serves as an informative finding in itself. It underscores the likely private nature of interactions among these influential figures and highlights the distinction between public discourse and confidential conversations within the highest echelons of finance and technology. The investigation reaffirms the profound Munger-Li Lu connection and Munger's nuanced, value-driven assessments within the electric vehicle sector, contrasting his views on Tesla and BYD.
