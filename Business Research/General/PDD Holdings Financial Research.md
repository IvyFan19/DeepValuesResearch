**2. Business Model and Revenue Generation:**

PDD Holdings operates primarily through its e-commerce platforms, Pinduoduo in China and Temu in the global market.<sup>1</sup> The core of its business model lies in social shopping, effectively merging the functionalities of online retail with social media engagement.<sup>3</sup> A distinctive feature is the "team purchase" model, which incentivizes users to form buying groups to unlock lower prices, leveraging network effects for customer acquisition and engagement.<sup>1</sup> This approach fosters a more interactive and engaging shopping experience, encouraging viral marketing as users share product information and invite others to join their shopping teams.<sup>3</sup> The company's initial strategy focused on affordability, offering deep discounts and even occasional free items, which proved particularly successful in attracting a large user base in remote locations before expanding to urban markets to compete with established players like Alibaba and JD.com on price.<sup>2</sup>

The integration of social media functionalities within the shopping process is seamless on PDD's platforms, allowing users to easily create or join shopping teams, share product information across various social networks, and manage purchases collectively.<sup>3</sup> This social e-commerce strategy significantly reduces marketing costs and enhances consumer engagement.<sup>4</sup> Furthermore, PDD Holdings has made substantial investments in strengthening its market presence by reducing service fees and mandatory deposits for merchants, as well as lowering commissions for installment purchases for customers.<sup>1</sup> These measures aim to make the platform more attractive to both sellers and buyers, fostering a positive feedback loop of increased trade volume and customer demand.<sup>1</sup> The company also focuses on improving after-sales service and simplifying the process of withdrawing funds for merchants, building trust and confidence within its ecosystem.<sup>1</sup>

PDD Holdings generates revenue through several key streams. One primary source is **online marketing services**, where merchants pay fees for priority listings and advertisements on the platform to enhance their visibility and reach a broader customer base.<sup>2</sup> This includes strategies like sponsored products and other forms of digital marketing.<sup>3</sup> Another significant and increasingly important revenue stream comes from **transaction services**, where PDD charges a commission fee for each item sold through its online platforms.<sup>2</sup> This revenue source has seen substantial growth, particularly with the rapid expansion of Temu.<sup>6</sup> Additionally, the company earns through **service fees** and **merchant and marketing services**, where an online bidding system determines the price and placement of items on the platform.<sup>2</sup> Historically, PDD's revenue model leaned more heavily on advertising income, especially in its early stages when focusing on ultra-low prices.<sup>5</sup> However, the current trend indicates a shift towards a more balanced model with a growing proportion of revenue derived from transaction fees, reflecting the increasing sales volume on its platforms.<sup>6</sup> In 2024, revenue from transaction services is projected to surge, with Temu contributing a significant portion, estimated to be around 70%.<sup>6</sup> This evolution suggests a maturing business model capable of effectively monetizing the transactions occurring on its marketplaces.

**4. Intrinsic Value Estimation:**

Warren Buffett's method for calculating intrinsic value fundamentally revolves around estimating the present value of a company's future free cash flows. This involves several key steps, starting with projecting the company's future earnings per share (EPS). Following this, a reasonable growth rate for these earnings over a defined period, typically ten years, needs to be estimated. Determining an appropriate discount rate is also crucial, often derived from the company's cost of capital, which takes into account the risk-free rate and a risk premium associated with the specific company and its industry. Once these projections are made, the present value of each year's estimated free cash flow is calculated using the determined discount rate. Finally, a terminal value, representing the value of the company beyond the projection period, is estimated and also discounted back to its present value. The sum of the present values of all future free cash flows and the terminal value constitutes the estimated intrinsic value of the company.

To apply this method to PDD Holdings, relevant financial data is necessary. This includes the company's current earnings per share, which can be found in its financial reports (refer to sections 8 and 9, although the snippets do not contain this specific data). Estimating a future growth rate requires careful consideration of analyst expectations, which suggest strong growth in gross merchandise value and revenue.<sup>6</sup> However, adopting a conservative approach necessitates using a more moderate and sustainable long-term growth rate to account for potential economic fluctuations, competitive pressures, and the inherent uncertainties of forecasting. The snippets do mention expected earnings and revenue growth <sup>7</sup>, which will be valuable in forming this conservative estimate. Determining an appropriate discount rate for PDD Holdings will involve analyzing its cost of capital, considering factors such as the market risk, the specific risks associated with its business operations in the e-commerce sector and its international presence, and the prevailing interest rates in the relevant markets. By employing a lower growth rate than optimistic analyst projections and a reasonable discount rate that reflects the associated risks, a conservative estimate of PDD Holdings' intrinsic value can be derived. Furthermore, considering different scenarios with varying growth rate assumptions can help establish a range of potential intrinsic values, providing a more nuanced perspective.
