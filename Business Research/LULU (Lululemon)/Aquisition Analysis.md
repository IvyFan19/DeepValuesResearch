### **How to read the list**

-   **Stars (\*)** ≠ imminent sale, but each has **documented strategic‑option activity** (bankers hired, Reuters leak, CEO hints, or rumored PE term sheets).

-   **Market‑cap filter**: $1 B - $50 B sweet‑spot where premiums are digestible for Big Tech/PE and antitrust is manageable.

-   **Sector spread** mimics Slack‑LinkedIn‑Blizzard‑Arm‑Xilinx diversity: collaboration SaaS, data/AI infra, gaming/content, and semiconductor IP.

### **Keep in mind**

-   **Regulators can still kill "obvious" fits** (Nvidia‑Arm). Chip deals or anything involving cloud‑gaming or AI infrastructure will invite Phase‑2 reviews in the U.S., EU, UK and, increasingly, China.

-   **Premium math**: If history rhymes, expect 25‑40 % over 30‑day VWAP for clean software deals; semis tend toward &gt;40 % to reflect CFIUS/geopolitical risk.

-   **Private‑equity dry‑powder** ($2 T globally) often wins over strategic buyers when Big Tech fears antitrust optics (see Smartsheet 2024 take‑private).

Use this slate as a **watch‑list**, not as investment advice. A sudden AI‑platform land‑grab, a regulatory change, or a big‑tech earnings miss could reshuffle probabilities quickly.
