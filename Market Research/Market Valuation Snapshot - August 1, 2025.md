\#\# 1. What These Numbers Mean

\#\#\# Buffett Indicator

\* Compares the total market value of all U.S. equities to the size of the real economy.

\* Historically \*\*\\\~100 %\*\* ≈ "fair value." Readings \*\*&gt;150 %\*\* have preceded periods of below‑average 10‑yr equity returns.

\#\#\# Shiller CAPE

\* Uses \*10‑year\*, inflation‑adjusted earnings to smooth the business cycle.

\* The 20‑year average is \\\~27. A print of \*\*38\*\* ranks in the \*\*top decile\*\* of post‑WWII history, hinting at \*\*compressed forward returns\*\*.
